## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Breathedge 2**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life controls for Breathedge 2's survival and exploration systems. It can help manage oxygen, hunger, thirst, fatigue, health, equipment durability, resources, crafting, and combat while preserving the game's core first-person space-survival experience.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore, complete quests, experiment with crafting and DIY upgrades, or reduce the pressure of oxygen, survival needs, durability, resources, and combat without changing the game's story content.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Oxygen** — Prevents the suit's oxygen supply from decreasing during open-space exploration.
- **Survival Needs Lock** — Locks hunger, thirst, fatigue, and related survival values at safe levels.
- **God Mode** — Protects the Man from environmental damage, hazards, and ordinary sources of injury.
- **Infinite Equipment Durability** — Stops tools, armor, and equipped DIY modules from losing durability during use.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Resources** — Keeps gathered crafting materials available while collecting, dismantling, repairing, and building.
- **Easy Crafting** — Allows crafting and equipment upgrades without consuming normal resource quantities.

### 🌍 World

- **Instant Repairs** — Completes repair interactions immediately and avoids normal material consumption.

### 🚶 Movement

- **No Fatigue** — Prevents physical tasks, object manipulation, and extended activity from increasing fatigue.
- **Increased Movement Speed** — Adjusts movement speed to make travel through dense exploration areas faster.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Damage Multiplier** — Increases outgoing damage from available weapons and melee equipment.
- **Infinite Ammunition** — Prevents ammunition or attack charges from being consumed when supported by the equipped weapon.
- **Reduced Enemy Damage** — Lowers incoming damage from hostile robots and other combat encounters.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Start the game and load a save before enabling movement options. Use increased movement speed for faster travel through open space and dense locations, and use the no-fatigue option when performing repeated physical tasks or moving objects. Disable movement changes before scripted sequences or when precise positioning is required.

### Tools & Inventory

Enable unlimited resources before gathering, dismantling, repairing, or crafting. Easy crafting is intended for testing blueprints, tools, equipment, armor, and DIY upgrades. Items still need to be obtained normally unless the trainer's resource option is active. Save before experimenting with large inventories or unusual item combinations.

### World & Environment

Use unlimited oxygen when leaving a safe area to explore wrecks, stations, settlements, and other locations. Survival needs lock can maintain oxygen-related preparation, hunger, thirst, and fatigue values while exploring. Instant repairs is useful for interactive machinery and damaged equipment, but story progression and quest objectives must still be completed normally.

### Combat & Weapons

Enable god mode or reduced enemy damage before entering a hostile area. Damage multiplier improves attacks from supported weapons and melee equipment, while infinite ammunition prevents supported ammunition values from decreasing. Turn combat options off before scripted encounters if an event does not advance correctly.
